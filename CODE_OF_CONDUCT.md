# Code of conduct

Clasp welcomes respectful participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behaviour

- Be constructive, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's sensitive data.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about product scope, security, permissions, and project boundaries.
- Give credit and follow dependency and asset licences.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately introducing malicious code, hidden data collection, credential access, or deceptive behaviour.
- Impersonating Nothing Technology Limited or presenting Clasp as an official Nothing product.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately using the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the community and project.
